# Customer Feedback Request Email Template

## Purpose

To understand how satisfied the customer was with the support experience and gather feedback that can help improve service quality.

## When to Use

- A customer's issue has been resolved
- A support ticket has been closed
- The customer has confirmed that the issue has been resolved

## Template

Subject: We'd Love Your Feedback

Hello [Customer Name],

We are glad to know that your issue has been resolved.

We would appreciate it if you could take a moment to rate your support experience and share your feedback with us.

Your feedback helps us improve our support processes and continue providing better service to our customers.

[Feedback Survey Link]

Thank you for choosing [Company Name]. We appreciate the opportunity to assist you.

Kind regards,

Customer Support Team

## Notes

- Keep the survey short and easy to complete.
- Send the feedback request shortly after the issue is resolved.
- Include a survey or feedback link where applicable.
- Thank the customer for their time and participation.
