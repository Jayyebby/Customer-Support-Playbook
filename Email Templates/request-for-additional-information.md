# Request for Additional Information Email Template

## Purpose

To request information needed to investigate and resolve a customer's issue.

## When to Use

- Additional details are required to troubleshoot an issue
- Customer report is incomplete
- Verification is needed before proceeding

## Template

Subject: Additional Information Required for Your Support Request

Hello [Customer Name],

Thank you for contacting [Company Name].

We are currently reviewing your request and require some additional information to assist you further.

Please provide the following:

- [Information Required]
- [Information Required]
- [Information Required]

Once we receive this information, we will continue our investigation and work toward resolving the issue.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Kind regards,

Customer Support Team

## Notes

- Be specific about the information needed.
- Avoid requesting unnecessary information.
