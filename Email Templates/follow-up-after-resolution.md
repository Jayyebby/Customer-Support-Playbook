# Follow-Up After Resolution Email Template

## Purpose

To confirm that the customer's issue has been fully resolved and to ensure they are satisfied with the support received.

## When to Use

- A support ticket has been marked as resolved
- A technical issue has been fixed
- A customer request has been completed

## Template

Subject: Following Up on Your Recent Support Request

Hello [Customer Name],

We recently resolved the issue associated with your support request and wanted to follow up to ensure everything is working as expected.

If you are still experiencing any difficulties or have additional questions, please let us know and we will be happy to assist.

If everything has been resolved to your satisfaction, no further action is required.

Thank you for choosing [Company Name]. We appreciate the opportunity to assist you.

Kind regards,

Customer Support Team

## Notes

- Send within 24–72 hours after resolving the issue.
- Keep the message brief and customer-focused.
- Provide a clear opportunity for the customer to reopen the conversation if needed.
