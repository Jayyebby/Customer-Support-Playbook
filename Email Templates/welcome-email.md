# Welcome Email Template

## Purpose

To welcome new customers after they create an account or sign up for a product or service.

## When to Use

- Customer creates a new account
- Customer subscribes to a service
- Customer purchases a product requiring onboarding

## Template

Subject: Welcome to [Company Name]

Hello [Customer Name],

Welcome to [Company Name].

We're excited to have you join us and look forward to helping you get the most out of our products and services.

To get started, we recommend:

- Completing your profile
- Exploring the available features
- Reviewing our help resources and documentation

If you have any questions, our support team is here to help.

Thank you for choosing [Company Name].

Kind regards,

Customer Support Team

## Notes

- Personalize the customer name before sending.
- Include relevant onboarding resources where applicable.
- Ensure all links are working before deployment.
