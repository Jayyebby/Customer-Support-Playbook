# Issue Resolved Email Template

## Purpose

To inform a customer that their reported issue has been resolved.

## When to Use

- Technical issue fixed
- Account issue resolved
- Customer request completed

## Template

Subject: Your Issue Has Been Resolved

Hello [Customer Name],

We are pleased to inform you that the issue you reported has been resolved.

Issue Reference: [Ticket Number]

Please review the service and confirm that everything is working as expected.

If you continue to experience any difficulties or have additional questions, please let us know and we will be happy to assist.

Thank you for your patience throughout this process.

Kind regards,

Customer Support Team

## Notes

- Encourage customer verification.
- Leave the conversation open for further assistance.
