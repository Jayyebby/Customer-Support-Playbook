# Delayed Response Apology Email Template

## Purpose

To acknowledge a delay in responding to a customer and maintain trust through transparent communication.

# When to Use

- Response time exceeded expectations
- High ticket volume caused delays
- Internal investigation required additional time

## Template

Subject: Apology for the Delay in Responding

Hello [Customer Name],

Thank you for your patience.

We sincerely apologize for the delay in responding to your request. We understand the importance of resolving your concern promptly and regret any inconvenience this may have caused.

Our team is currently reviewing your case and we will provide an update as soon as possible.

We appreciate your understanding and patience while we work toward a resolution.

Kind regards,

Customer Support Team

## Notes

- Acknowledge the delay.
- Avoid making promises that cannot be met.
