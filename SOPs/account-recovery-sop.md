# SOP: Account Recovery Process

## Purpose

To provide a standardized process for handling customer account recovery requests while ensuring account security and data protection.

---

## Scope

Applies to all cases involving lost, inaccessible, compromised, or deleted customer accounts.

---

## Procedure

1. Receive and acknowledge request

- Confirm receipt of account recovery request
- Reassure customer that the issue is being handled
- Do not guarantee recovery at this stage

---

2. Collect required information

Request:

- Registered email/phone number
- Username or account ID
- Recent activity or login history
- Description of issue

---

3. Classify the issue

Determine category:

- Forgotten credentials
- Suspended/locked account
- Deleted account
- Compromised account (security risk)

---

4. Attempt standard recovery

- Password reset flow
- Account recovery form submission
- Identity verification steps (if applicable)

---

5. Escalation rules

Escalate to Security/Technical team if:

- Account shows signs of unauthorized access
- Customer cannot verify ownership
- Account is permanently deleted
- Recovery requires backend system intervention

---

6. Security considerations

- Never disclose sensitive account data
- Follow strict identity verification protocols
- Prioritize account protection over speed

---

7. Communication with customer

- Provide status updates
- Explain next steps clearly
- Give realistic timelines
- Maintain calm and professional tone

---

## Notes

- Account recovery is a high-security process
- Verification is more important than speed
- Escalation is normal and expected
- Always prioritize data protection and trust
