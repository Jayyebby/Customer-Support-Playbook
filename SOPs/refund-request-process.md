# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Refund Request Handling

## Purpose

To ensure all customer refund requests are handled consistently, fairly, and in line with company policy.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to all customer support agents handling refund-related inquiries.

---

## Procedure

1. Receive and acknowledge request

- Confirm receipt of the refund request
- Respond politely and acknowledge the customer’s concern
- Avoid approving or denying immediately

---

2. Collect required information

Request the following details:

- Order or transaction ID
- Date of purchase
- Reason for refund request
- Any supporting evidence (if applicable)

---

3. Verify customer eligibility

- Check refund policy guidelines
- Confirm if request falls within allowed refund period
- Review purchase and account details
- If unclear, escalate to senior support or supervisor

---

4. Make a decision

If eligible:

- Approve refund request
- Inform customer of approval

If not eligible:

- Politely explain reason for rejection
- Provide alternative support options where possible

---

5. Process refund (if approved)

- Submit refund request through the system
- Confirm processing timeline with customer
- Update ticket status

---

6. Close the ticket

- Confirm resolution with customer
- Ask if further assistance is needed
- Mark ticket as resolved in the system

---

## Communication Guidelines

- Always remain polite and professional
- Avoid blaming the customer
- Use clear and simple language
- Show empathy at every stage

---

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to senior support if:

- Policy is unclear
- High-value refund request
- Repeated complaints from customer

---

## Notes

- Follow policy before making decisions
- Consistency is critical in refund handling
- Escalation is better than guessing
- Customer experience should remain respectful even in rejection cases
