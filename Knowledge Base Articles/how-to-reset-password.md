# How to Reset Your Password

## Overview

This article explains how to reset your password if you are unable to log in to your account.

---

## When to Use This Guide

Use this guide if:

- You forgot your password
- You are unable to log in
- Your password is not working

---

## Steps to Reset Your Password

1. Go to the login page

Open the application or website and click on the Login button.

---

2. Click on “Forgot Password”

Select the Forgot Password option below the login form.

---

3. Enter your registered email

Input the email address associated with your account and submit the request.

---

4. Check your email inbox

You will receive a password reset email. If you don’t see it:

- Check your spam or junk folder

---

5. Create a new password

Click the link in the email and follow the instructions to set a new password.

---

6. Log in with your new password

Return to the login page and sign in using your new credentials.

---

Troubleshooting

If you do not receive the reset email:

- Ensure you entered the correct email address
- Wait a few minutes and check again
- Try resending the request

If the issue continues, contact support.

---

## Notes

- Always use a strong password
- Keep your login email updated
- Use the reset feature whenever you cannot access your account
