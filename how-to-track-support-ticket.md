# How to Track Your Support Ticket

## Overview

This article explains how you can track the progress of your support request after it has been submitted.

---

## When to Use This Guide

Use this guide if:

- You have submitted a support request
- You want to check the status of your issue
- You need updates on an ongoing ticket

---

## How to Track Your Ticket

1. Check your confirmation email

After submitting a support request, you will receive a confirmation email containing:

- Your ticket number
- A tracking link (if available)

---

2. Use the ticket tracking link

Click on the link provided in your email to view:

- Current ticket status (Open, In Progress, Resolved)
- Assigned support agent (if applicable)
- Updates and communication history

---

3. Log in to your support portal (if available)

Some platforms allow you to:

- Log in to your account
- View all submitted tickets
- Track progress in real time

---

4. Check email updates

You may also receive automated updates when:

- Your ticket is assigned
- Progress is made
- Your issue is resolved

---

## What the Ticket Status Means

- Open: Your request has been received but not yet reviewed
- In Progress: A support agent is currently working on it
- Pending: Waiting for additional information from you
- Resolved: The issue has been fixed or closed

---

## What to Do If You Cannot Find Your Ticket

If you cannot track your ticket:

- Check your email spam folder
- Confirm you used the correct email address
- Contact support with your ticket number

---

## Notes

- Always save your ticket number
- Use the tracking link for real-time updates
- Keep an eye on email notifications
