# Customer Refund Request Scenario

## Scenario

A customer contacts support requesting a refund due to dissatisfaction with a product or service, billing issues, or unmet expectations.

---

## Objective

To evaluate the refund request based on company policy, communicate clearly with the customer, and ensure a fair and professional resolution.

---

## Response Process

1. Acknowledge the request and show empathy

- Thank the customer for reaching out
- Acknowledge their concern without immediately approving or denying the refund

2. Gather necessary details

Ask for relevant information such as:

- Order number or transaction ID
- Date of purchase
- Reason for refund request

3. Review eligibility (based on policy)

- Check refund policy guidelines
- Determine if the request meets refund conditions
- If unclear, escalate for approval

4. Communicate the decision

If approved:

- Confirm refund approval
- Provide timeline for processing

If not approved:

- Explain reason politely
- Offer possible alternatives (support, troubleshooting, credits, etc.)

5. Close professionally

- Reassure customer of continued support
- Invite further questions if needed

---

## Sample Response

Hello [Customer Name],

Thank you for contacting us regarding your refund request.

We’re sorry to hear that our product/service did not meet your expectations.

To help us process your request, could you please provide the following details:

- Order/Transaction ID
- Date of purchase
- Reason for requesting a refund

Once we receive this information, we will review your request based on our refund policy and get back to you with an update as soon as possible.

We appreciate your patience and understanding.

Kind regards,
Customer Support Team

---

## Notes

- Always remain neutral and professional
- Never approve or deny refunds without checking policy
- Show empathy even when denying requests
- Offer alternatives when refunds are not possible
