# Customer Cannot Log In Scenario

## Scenario

A customer reports that they are unable to log into their account.

---

## Objective

To identify the cause of the login issue and guide the customer toward regaining access to their account.

---

## Response Process

1. Acknowledge the issue

Start by apologizing and reassuring the customer that the issue will be addressed.

2. Ask diagnostic questions

- Are you receiving any error message when trying to log in?
- Are you using the correct email address associated with your account?
- Have you recently changed or reset your password?

3. Provide troubleshooting steps (while awaiting response)

- Use the “Forgot Password” option to reset your password
- Clear browser cache or app data and try again
- Try logging in using a different browser or device
- Ensure internet connection is stable

4. Request customer confirmation

Ask the customer to try logging in again after completing the steps and provide feedback on the result.

5. Escalation (if issue persists)

If the issue is not resolved, escalate to the technical team for further investigation.

---

## Sample Response

Hello [Customer Name],

We’re sorry you are experiencing difficulty logging into your account.

To help us assist you better, could you please confirm the following:

- Are you receiving any error message when attempting to log in?
- Which email address are you using?
- Have you recently changed or reset your password?

While we look into this, please try the following steps:

- Use the “Forgot Password” option to reset your password
- Clear your browser cache and try again
- Attempt logging in using another browser or device

Once done, please let us know if the issue persists so we can assist further.

Kind regards,
Customer Support Team

---

## Notes 

- Always acknowledge the customer’s frustration first
- Ask clear and specific diagnostic questions
- Provide guided troubleshooting steps
- Escalate when necessary instead of guessing
