# Angry Customer Complaint Scenario

## Scenario

A customer contacts support in an angry or frustrated state due to an unresolved issue, delay, or negative experience.

---

## Objective

To de-escalate the situation, acknowledge the customer’s frustration, identify the issue, and work toward a resolution while maintaining professionalism.

---

## Response Process

1. Acknowledge, show empathy and apologies sincerely

- Recognize the customer’s frustration
- Avoid being defensive
- Use calm and respectful language
- Apologize for the inconvenience caused
- Do not over-explain at this stage

2. Allow the customer to explain the issue

- Let the customer fully express their concern
- Do not interrupt or assume details

3. Clarify the issue

Ask clear questions to understand the problem:

- What exactly happened?
- When did the issue occur?
- Have they encountered this before?

4. Provide a solution or next step

- Offer a clear resolution if available
- If not, explain what will happen next
- Provide timelines where possible

5. Reassure the customer

- Confirm that their issue is being taken seriously
- Ensure follow-up if escalation is required

---

## Sample Response

Hello [Customer Name],

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience and frustration this situation has caused you.

We understand how upsetting this must be, and we truly appreciate you bringing it to our attention.

To better assist you, could you please provide a few more details about the issue and when it occurred?

We want to make sure we fully understand what happened so we can resolve it properly.

Once we have this information, we will prioritize your case and ensure it is handled as quickly as possible.

Thank you for your patience and understanding.

Kind regards,
Customer Support Team

---

## Notes

- Always remain calm and professional regardless of customer tone
- Empathy comes before problem-solving
- Never argue or defend the company
- Focus on resolution, not blame
