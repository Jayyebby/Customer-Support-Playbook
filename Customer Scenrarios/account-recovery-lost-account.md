# Lost Account / Account Recovery Scenario

## Scenario

A customer reports that they have lost access to their account. This may be due to forgotten login details, accidental deletion, compromised account, or inability to verify ownership.

---

## Objective

To verify account ownership, assist with recovery where possible, and escalate to the appropriate security or technical team when necessary.

---

## Response Process

1. Acknowledge and reassure

- Respond calmly and professionally
- Reassure the customer that account recovery is a priority

---

2. Gather initial information

Request key details such as:

- Registered email or phone number
- Username or account ID
- Approximate date of account creation or last access
- Any error messages or account status noticed

---

3. Identify type of account issue

Determine if it is:

- Forgotten login credentials
- Suspended or locked account
- Deleted account
- Suspected unauthorized access

---

4. Provide initial recovery steps

Depending on platform:

- Password reset process
- Account recovery form
- Identity verification steps

---

5. Escalate when necessary

Escalate to technical/security team if:

- Account appears compromised
- Customer cannot verify ownership through standard steps
- Account has been deleted or permanently disabled
- High-risk security concern is detected

---

6. Communicate next steps

Inform the customer clearly about:

- What is being done
- Expected response time
- Any additional verification required

---

## Sample Response

Hello [Customer Name],

Thank you for contacting us.

We understand how concerning it can be to lose access to your account, and we’re here to help.

To assist you further, could you please provide:

- The email address or username linked to your account
- Any details about when you last had access
- Any error messages you are seeing

Once we receive this information, we will guide you through the appropriate recovery steps or escalate your case to our specialist team if needed.

We appreciate your patience and will do our best to help you regain access.

Kind regards,
Customer Support Team

---

## Notes

- Account recovery involves verification, not assumptions
- Security and ownership verification are critical
- Some cases require escalation to specialized teams
- Clear communication reduces customer frustration
