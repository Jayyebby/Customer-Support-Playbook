# App Crash Issue Scenario

## Scenario

A customer reports that the application crashes immediately after opening or during usage. This may occur on login, during navigation, or while performing a specific action.

---

## Objective

To identify possible causes of the app crash, guide the customer through basic troubleshooting steps, and escalate to the technical team if necessary.

---

## Response Process

1. Acknowledge and reassure

- Apologize for the inconvenience
- Reassure the customer that the issue will be investigated

---

2. Gather key information

Request:

- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- App version
- When the crash occurs (on launch, login, or specific action)
- Any error message (if visible)

---

3. Basic troubleshooting steps

Ask the customer to try:

- Restarting the device
- Updating the app to the latest version
- Clearing app cache/data
- Reinstalling the app
- Checking internet connection

---

4. Check for patterns

Determine if:

- The issue is device-specific
- The issue is app-version related
- The issue affects multiple users (possible system bug)

---

5. Escalation

Escalate to technical team if:

- Crash persists after troubleshooting
- Multiple users report same issue
- Issue appears linked to latest update
- Logs or backend investigation is required

---

6. Communicate next steps

- Inform customer if issue is being escalated
- Provide expected update timeline
- Reassure them that their case is being tracked

---

## Sample Response

Hello [Customer Name],

We’re sorry to hear that the app is crashing, and we understand how frustrating this can be.

To help us investigate, could you please provide:

- Your device model and operating system version
- The app version you are currently using
- When exactly the crash occurs (on launch, login, or during use)

In the meantime, please try the following steps:

- Restart your device
- Update the app to the latest version
- Clear the app cache or reinstall the app

If the issue continues, we will escalate this to our technical team for further investigation.

We appreciate your patience and understanding.

Kind regards,
Customer Support Team

---

## Notes

- App crashes require structured troubleshooting
- Information gathering is critical before escalation
- Some issues may indicate system-wide bugs
- Clear communication reduces customer frustration
